You are now at the deploy step, which is the last step in your CD pipeline. For this step, you will use the OpenShift client to deploy your Docker image to an OpenShift cluster.

Knowing that you want to deploy to OpenShift, you search Tekton Catalog for \"openshift\" and you see there is a task called `openshift-client` that will execute OpenShift commands on your cluster. You decide to use the openshift-client task in your pipeline to deploy your image.
